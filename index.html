FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before your first campaign.

What is LED screen advertising?
Your ad (image or video) plays on loop on bright LED display screens placed in busy indoor locations like restaurants, shops and waiting areas. People nearby see it repeatedly through the day, building strong local recall for your brand.
How much does it cost?
Plans start at ₹499. The exact price depends on the city, screen location, ad duration and how long you want to run the campaign. Message us on WhatsApp with your budget and we'll suggest the best plan.
What ad formats do you accept?
Images (JPG/PNG) and short videos (MP4). If you don't have a ready creative, we can design one for you — just share your logo, offer details and contact number.
How quickly can my ad go live?
Usually the same day. Once you confirm your plan and share the creative, we schedule it on the screens and send you proof that it's playing.
How do I know my ad is actually playing?
We share proof-of-play — photos and short videos of your ad running on the screens — so you have complete transparency on what you paid for.
Can I advertise in a city not listed here?
We're expanding every month. Message us on WhatsApp with your city and we'll let you know when screens go live there — or work out a custom option for you.
